The font

The classic 5 × 7 dot-matrix alphabet. Bulb number = row × 5 + column, counting from 0 at the top left. Each letter lights between 11 bulbs (I, J, L, T, Y) and 20 (B). Click a letter to hold it on the sign.

The box

336 × 456 × 110 mm. The face covers the front edges of the walls, so from the front you see only black acrylic and bulbs. Everything you might need to service comes out the back.

Electronics

An ESP32-C3 is enough: three pins drive a chain of five shift registers, one per relay board. The relays switch the hot side of each socket. All sockets share one neutral.

Before you build

Measure a socket

The socket size is copied from the MakeSign project. Measure yours and update the socket values in cad/marquee_matrix.py before cutting acrylic.

Test for ghosting

Zero-cross SSRs leak about 1 mA when off, which can make LED bulbs glow faintly. Wire one bulb to one channel first. If it glows, add a 0.1 µF X2 capacitor in series with 1 kΩ across the socket, or switch to mechanical relay boards.

Mains wiring

Fuse the line, strain-relieve the cord, cover every exposed 120 V terminal, and keep mains and 5 V wiring on separate sides of the bay. Bad connections are the real fire risk.

About acrylic and fire

Acrylic burns about as readily as wood, and it melts and drips. It's a good choice for laser cutting, not for fire safety. LED filament bulbs run cool, so careful wiring matters far more than the panel material.
